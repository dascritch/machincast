# Help on the list of feeds

![The Feeds tab, its elements numbered as below](../../../fr/captures/aide/liste-feeds.png)

The Feeds tab gives all the feeds, the reusable stream addresses, by name.
The numbers refer to the picture; **Help → Contextual help**, or <kbd>F1</kbd>, gives each of these helps as a tooltip, on the element itself.

1. **Filter…** (`filter_`) : keeps only the rows where a column contains the typed text, ignoring case and accents.
2. **Count** (`count`) : how many rows remain, out of how many.
3. **New feed** (`new`) : opens an empty card; nothing is in the database before **Save**.
4. **List** (`table`) : name, address and number of streams that follow it. The right-click offers the pre-listen of the feed, and to cut the air with it. A click on a column title sorts, a double-click or <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the card, a right-click on the titles chooses the columns.
